## Design Context

### Creator
- **Name**: Ramya (`a project by Ramya`)

### Concept & Purpose
- **Product**: Type Loom — A tactile, generative kinetic typography engine and poster art studio.
- **Design Metaphor**: Modern digital weaving loom meeting Figma's precision vector craft.
- **Core Aesthetic**: Framed floating studio artboard, sleek Figma-inspired bottom glassmorphism dock, vibrant multi-harmonic colorways, and real-time physical strings.

### Typography
- **Display Typeface**: `Fraunces` / `Syne` — Warm, variable modern display typography with tactile character.
- **UI & Controls**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` & `JetBrains Mono` for high-contrast accessibility (WCAG AA/AAA).

### Figma Color Ecosystem
- **Figma Prism**: `#F24E1E` (Figma Red), `#FF7262` (Figma Coral), `#A259FF` (Figma Purple), `#1ABCFE` (Figma Blue), `#0ACF83` (Figma Green).
- **Additional Palettes**: Electric Tokyo, Sunset Gradient, Neo Botanical, Deep Cyber, Monochrome Luxe.

### Direct Manipulation & Physics
- Strings dynamically sag and resonate using Verlet integration.
- Keyboard input: Typing weaves, Spacebar snips yarn with dangling physics, Backspace unravels, Enter clears canvas.
- Mouse interaction: Strumming / plucking across strings generates acoustic synthesized audio tones.
